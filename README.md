A predictive analytics project using Python and Power BI

**Status:** Complete

## Overview
A dashboard and risk model to help identify students at risk of
dropping out after their first semester. See [project brief](docs/project_brief.md).

## Key Findings
- 32.1% of the 4,424 students in the dataset dropped out.
- Students behind on tuition dropped out at 86.6%, compared with 24.7% for those up to date.
- More than 3 in 4 students who passed fewer than half their first-semester courses dropped out, compared with 12% of those who passed 75% or more.
- Dropout rates were highest among students aged 26 to 30 (59.8%) and lowest among those aged 17 to 20 (21.2%).
- 275 of 794 currently enrolled students score above the at-risk threshold and are listed for advisor outreach.
